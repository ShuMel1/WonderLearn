Common questions

How do I add another child?

Tap the profile button in the top-right corner of the home screen, then "Add child." Each child keeps their own progress on the device.

How do I change the language my child is learning?

Open the profile menu (top-right on the home screen) and choose the language you speak and the language you're learning — Armenian, English, or Russian.

What is the lock button for?

The lock keeps your child inside the app so they can't wander off to other apps or settings. To unlock, a grown-up answers a simple question. On iPhone this uses Guided Access; the app shows you how to turn it on and off.

Does Wisekins have ads or collect data?

No. There are no ads, no accounts, and no tracking. Everything your child does stays on the device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I remove a child's data?

Remove an individual profile from the app's settings, or uninstall the app to delete everything from the device.
