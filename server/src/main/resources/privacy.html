🦉 Wisekins — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Wisekins is a language-learning app for children. We built it to be safe and private by design. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Wisekins has no accounts, no login, and no sign-up.

  • A child's profile name, chosen languages, learning progress, streaks and daily goals are stored only on your device. This information never leaves the device and is never sent to us.
  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

The one thing we do send to our servers is anonymous, aggregate usage — for example, which in-app game was opened. Each event includes only a random identifier generated on the device (never linked to a name, profile, account, or device identifier), the app version and the platform. We use it solely to learn which activities children enjoy so we can improve the app. It contains no personal information and cannot be traced back to your child.

Our servers

The app downloads its lesson content (words, categories and images) from our servers. These are ordinary download requests. Like any web server, our servers may record standard technical log entries such as the request time and the device's IP address, used solely to operate and secure the service. The app also sends the anonymous usage events described above to our servers. We do not use any of this information to identify you or your child, and we do not build profiles from it.

Microphone and the speaking game

Wisekins includes a speaking game where a child says a word out loud and the app checks the pronunciation. This is the only part of the app that uses the microphone.

  • The microphone is used only while the speaking game is open, and only after the child taps the microphone button. The app never listens in the background.
  • Your device asks for microphone permission the first time it is needed. If you decline, the rest of the app continues to work normally.
  • We never receive your child's voice. Audio is not recorded to a file, is not stored, and is never sent to Wisekins servers.
  • Speech is turned into text by your device's own speech recognition — the same system used for dictation on the keyboard. This happens on the device whenever the device supports it.
  • If on-device recognition is unavailable for the chosen language, your device may send the audio to Apple's speech recognition service on iOS, or Google's on Android, to convert it to text. This is handled by the operating system under Apple's and Google's own privacy policies. You can avoid this entirely by not using the speaking game.
  • The app only receives the recognized text, compares it to the expected word on the device, and discards it. The comparison result is not stored beyond your child's normal progress (for example, that a word was answered correctly).

Children's privacy

Wisekins is designed for children and is compliant with children's privacy laws including COPPA and GDPR-K. We collect no personal information about your child. There is no behavioral advertising, no third-party ad or analytics SDKs, and no cross-app tracking anywhere in the app. The only data that reaches us is the anonymous usage described above, which cannot identify your child. Your child's voice is never sent to us, and the microphone is used only in the speaking game as described above.

Third parties

We do not use third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics providers, or tracking SDKs. The anonymous usage analytics described above are handled entirely by our own servers. We do not share, rent, or sell any information to anyone.

The one exception is your device's built-in speech recognition, used only in the speaking game as described above. When your device cannot recognize speech locally, it may send that audio to Apple or Google to convert it to text. We do not choose, control, or receive that audio — it is your device's own dictation system, governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies. No other third party receives anything through the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because all of your child's data stays on the device, uninstalling the app permanently removes it. You can also remove an individual child profile from within the app's settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
