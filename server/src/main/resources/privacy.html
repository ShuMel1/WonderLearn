🦉 WonderLearn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

WonderLearn is a language-learning app for children. We built it to be safe and private by design. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. WonderLearn has no accounts, no login, and no sign-up.

  • A child's profile name, chosen languages, learning progress, streaks and daily goals are stored only on your device. This information never leaves the device and is never sent to us.
  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Our servers

The app downloads its lesson content (words, categories and images) from our servers. These are ordinary download requests. Like any web server, our servers may record standard technical log entries such as the request time and the device's IP address, used solely to operate and secure the service. We do not use this information to identify you or your child, and we do not build profiles from it.

Children's privacy

WonderLearn is designed for children and is compliant with children's privacy laws including COPPA and GDPR-K. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing about your child for us to store, share, or sell. There is no behavioral advertising and no tracking anywhere in the app.

Third parties

We do not use third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking SDKs. We do not share, rent, or sell any information to anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Because all of your child's data stays on the device, uninstalling the app permanently removes it. You can also remove an individual child profile from within the app's settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy in WonderLearn, contact us at support@wonderlearn.app.
